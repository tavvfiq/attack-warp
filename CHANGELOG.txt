Attack Warp changelog

0.3.0 (2026-10-29)
- Stretch follows the gap still to close: none once inside the brake zone, full fMaxScale at fStretchRange (150) beyond it,
  smooth between. Close swings now play as animated instead of speeding up to 1.8x and braking hard (likely "snap" source).
- Turn: during wind-up and swing the attacker turns toward its target (fTurnRate 300 deg/s, spring fTurnEase 0.08 s, only
  within fMaxTurnAngle 90). The player is skipped while True Directional Movement steers (directional mode or lock-on).
- Lead: stretch and turn aim at where a moving target will be fLeadTime (0.25 s) ahead, at most fMaxLead (80) away. The brake
  still uses the target's real position.
- Combo target stickiness: the player's previous target keeps its pick unless another scores fTargetStickiness (0.3) better.
- True Directional Movement: its locked target is the player's target (bTDMTarget).
- Precision: the stop distance moves with the weapon's attack collision length (bPrecisionReach, fReachRef 60, fReachWeight 1),
  read once per swing on the main thread; never inside body contact (45). Without Precision: fixed distances as before.
- Power and charge attacks use fMaxStartDistPower (700) instead of fMaxStartDist.
- The player's target is held by handle for the frame (was a raw pointer looked up by FormID).
- Debug swing line adds: power, degrees turned, Precision length ("capsule"), target speed.

0.2.0 (2026-10-27)
- Stretch factor follows its target through a critically damped spring (after orangeduck's spring.h, MIT) instead of a
  first-order ease: same fStretchEase meaning (63% in that time), but no sudden acceleration when a stretch starts or ends.
- Brake factor is sprung too (new fBrakeEase, 0.05 s; 0 = 0.1.0 behaviour): when the target steps in or its body enters the
  path, the brake tapers instead of jumping. The hard clamp to the stop line still holds every frame.
- Load-time self-check covers the spring.

0.1.0 (2026-10-27)
- First version, built from MotionWarpProbe 0.2.2 (runs 1-4) with the user's feedback "sometimes it feels like snapping".
- Hook: MovementTweenerAgentAnimationDriven second vtable slot 3 (SE 41160 / AE 42246), forward root motion of melee attacks only.
- Stretch eased (fStretchEase 0.12 s) up to fMaxScale 1.8 during wind-up and swing (attack states draw/swing; ends one frame after HitFrame).
- Brake: forward path kept fStopDistPlayer 70 / fStopDistNpc 100 from the target centre, smooth taper over fBrakeZone 60.
- Player target per swing from hostile actors inside fTargetCone 60 deg (refreshed on combat events and while attacking).
- SKSE Menu Framework page, AttackWarp.ini + AttackWarp_User.ini, quiet log with optional per-swing lines, load-time math self-check.
